# Foundations of Neural Networks

## Level 1: One-Sentence Summary
The foundations of deep learning lie in mimicking biological neural structures to create computational units capable of logical reasoning and learning.

## Level 2: Intuition
Modern neural networks didn't emerge in a vacuum. They evolved from a desire to model the human brain's massive parallelism. The journey started with simple binary threshold units (MP Neurons) and progressed to units that could learn from data (Perceptrons).

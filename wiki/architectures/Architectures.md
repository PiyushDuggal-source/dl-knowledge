# Neural Network Architectures

## Level 1: One-Sentence Summary
Architecture refers to the structural arrangement of neurons and layers that determine how a model processes and transforms input data.

## Level 2: Intuition
Different problems require different "shapes" of models. While basic networks (MLPs) are great for simple tabular data, we need more specialized structures for images (CNNs) or sequences (RNNs/Transformers). This section tracks the evolution of these "blueprints."
